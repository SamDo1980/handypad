WHY CHOOSE HANDYPAD?

FIREPROOF CANVAS

Flame-retardant outer canvas for hot-works zones — welding, grinding, and cutting decks — without swapping the whole pad out.

CLOSED-CELL FOAM SLEEVE

Wraps couplers and tube ends so straps, tarps, and hands don't meet a sharp edge. Sewn, not glued, so it holds up through strip-downs and re-erects.

REFLECTIVE TAPE

3M-grade reflective strip stitched to the outer face. Keeps low-light and night-shift scaffold visible to plant, vehicles, and foot traffic.
